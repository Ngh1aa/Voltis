08 / CAREERS

Build the system, not just the vehicle.

Future VOLTIS teams will span product, engineering, digital experience, energy and communication. The common requirement is the ability to think beyond one discipline.

DISCIPLINES

Different expertise, shared standards.

Polestar organises careers by department while connecting each team back to vehicle performance. VOLTIS uses the same systems logic: every discipline contributes to one customer experience.

01 / PRODUCT

Vehicle Design

Exterior, interior, packaging, materials and physical interaction.

02 / DIGITAL

Experience

In-car software, websites, service flows and accessibility.

03 / ENGINEERING

Systems

Energy, electronics, validation and vehicle integration.

04 / ENERGY

Infrastructure

Charging, storage, site operations and energy communication.

HOW WE WORK

Small teams. Clear ownership. Shared evidence.

The prototype culture avoids vague “move fast” language. It defines how decisions are made and what good collaboration looks like.

01
Write it down.

Decisions, assumptions and open questions belong in a shared source of truth.

02
Prototype early.

Test behaviour and understanding before polishing presentation.

03
Show evidence.

Use research, testing and engineering context to resolve disagreement.

04
Own the handoff.

A task is not complete until the next person can continue without guessing.
