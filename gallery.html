VISUAL SYSTEM

Different subjects. One art direction.

High contrast, low visual clutter, restrained green and orange signals, and photography that shows real objects rather than decorative technology clichés.

ART DIRECTION

Photography should explain something.

Every image has a job: show form, reveal interaction, document engineering or provide energy context. Generic “future tech” imagery is intentionally excluded.

01 / FORMWide

Use landscape frames to explain proportion and movement.

02 / HUMANClose

Use people only where they explain interaction, scale or collaboration.

03 / SYSTEMReal

Show infrastructure as actual hardware rather than abstract glowing lines.
