A technical, restrained visual system for electric mobility: carbon fields, warm evidence surfaces, instrument indexing and a controlled energy rail.

Green represents energy and the primary next action. Orange represents motion, change and secondary peaks.

Logo

Forward motion,
reduced to a mark.

The VOLTIS mark stays geometric and compact. Use the green mark on carbon or the carbon mark on an Energy Green field. Avoid gradients, glow and decorative enclosure.

Motion principles

Kinetic,
never busy.

Motion communicates state, hierarchy or direction. Large continuous decoration is avoided and reduced-motion must remain fully usable.

Micro / 170msState.

Hover, press, focus and compact control feedback.

Normal / 260msTransition.

Navigation, filters and local layout transitions.

Reveal / 640–900msOrientation.

One-time hero or composition activation. Never block the task.
