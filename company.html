02 / COMPANY

Build less noise. Create more meaning.

VOLTIS is a concept for an electric mobility company that treats the vehicle, software, charging experience and brand as one connected system.

Our principlesExplore careers

Electric mobility should feel simpler because the technology is better — not more complicated because there is more technology.

MANIFESTO / 01

We use clarity as a design constraint. Every feature, message and interaction should earn its place by helping someone understand, decide or move with less friction.

That principle applies to product design, digital interfaces, energy communication and the way the company explains its environmental impact.

OPERATING MODEL

Designed as one connected organisation.

Lucid separates technology, design and user experience as distinct but connected product disciplines; VOLTIS adopts the same clarity of responsibility while keeping one visual and interaction system across them.

01 / PRODUCT

Vehicle + platform

Form, packaging, energy efficiency and physical controls are developed together instead of in separate visual silos.

02 / DIGITAL

Interface + service

In-car software, website journeys and customer support follow the same principles for language, hierarchy and feedback.

03 / ENERGY

Charging + infrastructure

Energy is treated as part of the ownership experience, not a technical topic pushed to documentation.

04 / IMPACT

Evidence + responsibility

Environmental claims should be supported by lifecycle evidence, sourcing context and clear boundaries around what is known.

VALUES / 04

Four behaviours shape every decision.

Not slogans for a wall — practical filters for product, communication and collaboration.

01 / CLARITY
Make it understandable.

If something cannot be explained clearly, it is not ready to be presented as simple.

02 / PURPOSE
Design with intent.

Every visual and interaction should support a user need, not fill space.

03 / EVIDENCE
Prove the claim.

Performance and sustainability statements require context, source and method.

04 / MOMENTUM
Move forward.

Progress comes from testing, learning and improving the system continuously.

ENGINEERING / COLLABORATION
HOW WE WORK

Cross-functional by default.

Polestar frames careers around departments while stressing that every component and person contributes to overall performance. VOLTIS applies that systems idea to project teams: product, design, engineering and communication work from one brief and one source of truth.
