DISCLOSURE STANDARD

Publish context, not just claims.

Every future performance or sustainability statement should include what was measured, how it was measured, the applicable product or market, and the date of the source.

A
Scope

State exactly which vehicle, component, project or lifecycle stage the claim covers.

B
Method

Explain the test, calculation or reporting framework in language people can understand.

C
Source

Link the primary evidence and show when it was last updated.
